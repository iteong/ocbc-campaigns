IMPORTANT NOTICE

Changes to OCBC 365 Credit Card cashback benefits from 1 November 2026

From 1 November 2026, the eligible cashback categories, cashback rates, minimum spend requirement and cashback cap for the OCBC 365 Credit Card will be revised.

Key updates include:

  • Cashback programme will be simplified to a single S$800 minimum spend requirement and S$160 monthly cashback cap.
  • Cashback on eligible Dining, Groceries and Land Transport transactions will increase to 6%.
  • Eligible Dining transactions will be expanded to include fast food restaurants.
  • Petrol transactions will continue to earn 6% cashback.
  • Cashback on Telco, Utilities, Streaming and EV Charging transactions will change to the base cashback rate of 0.25%.
  • Watsons transactions will continue to earn 3% cashback until 31 March 2027, after which the base cashback rate of 0.25% will apply.
  • Eligible advertising platform spend continues to earn up to 1% cashback, with no cashback cap and no minimum spend requirement.

For more details, please refer to our Frequently Asked Questions and revised Terms and Conditions.

When will I receive my cashback?

Cashback earned from transactions made by the Principal Cardmember and Supplementary Cardmembers will be credited to the Principal Cardmember’s OCBC 365 Credit Card account by the end of the following calendar month after the transaction is posted.

For example:

  • You make eligible transactions of S$500 on Dining and S$300 on Groceries in November 2026. As the minimum spend requirement of S$800 is met, if the transactions are posted by 30 November 2026, S$48 cashback will be credited to the Principal Cardmember’s OCBC 365 Credit Card account by 31 December 2026.
I made a transaction before 1 November 2026, but the transaction was posted on or after 1 November 2026. Which cashback rate will apply?

Cashback will be awarded based on the transaction's posting date, not the transaction date.

If your transaction is posted on or after 1 November 2026, the revised cashback rates and Terms and Conditions effective from 1 November 2026 will apply.

The posting date refers to the date the transaction is received by the card issuer, i.e. OCBC Bank. As merchants may submit transactions for settlement several days after the purchase is made, the posting date may differ from the transaction date. OCBC Bank is unable to control when a merchant submits a transaction for posting.

Dining

  • Dining cashback is applicable to spend at dining merchants locally and overseas with their main business activity classified as "Dining" which includes restaurants, cafes and caterers.
  • Online food delivery is applicable to online spend made at food delivery merchants specifically Deliveroo, foodpanda and GrabFood.
  • Where the local and overseas dining merchant is located within a hotel, bar or a country club, the main business activity of such merchant may not be classified as "Dining".
  • For the avoidance of doubt, the dining spend incurred at any merchant not classified as "Dining", such as spend on dining at bar and/or pubs as well as wedding banquets held at hotels and country clubs will not qualify for the dining cashback.

Groceries

  • Grocery cashback is applicable to spend at grocery merchants locally and overseas with their main business activity classified as "Grocery" only.
  • Online grocery is applicable to online spend made at grocery merchants with their main business activity classified as "Grocery". For example, FairPrice Online and RedMart.

Land transport

  • Land transport cashback is applicable to spend made at road and rail transport locally and overseas such as Grab (excluding GrabPay), Gojek, RYDE and ComfortDelGro.

Petrol

Petrol cashback is applicable to spend made at all petrol kiosks locally and overseas with their main business activity is classified as "Petrol".

For the avoidance of doubt, the petrol spend incurred through fleet cards (i.e. corporate charge cards that offer existing discounts) do not qualify for the petrol cashback.
